About the Show

Avatar: The Last Airbender is an animated series that first aired on Nickelodeon in 2005. It was created by Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko.

The world is divided into four nations. Some people in each nation can "bend" (control) one element. Only the Avatar can master all four elements and keep the world in balance.

One hundred years before the story starts, the Fire Nation began a war against the other nations. The Avatar disappeared. Then two siblings from the Southern Water Tribe find a boy frozen in an iceberg: Aang, the last airbender and the new Avatar.

The Four Nations

Water Tribes

Waterbenders move with the flow of water. Their power is strongest under the full moon. The tribes live at the North and South Poles.

Earth Kingdom

The largest nation. Earthbenders are strong and patient. Its capital is the great walled city of Ba Sing Se.

Fire Nation

Firebenders get their power from the sun and their inner energy. The Fire Lord rules the nation from the Caldera City.

Air Nomads

Peaceful monks who lived in four air temples. Airbenders are free and quick. Aang is the last of his people.

Main Characters

  • Aang – The 12-year-old Avatar and last airbender.
  • Katara – A waterbender from the Southern Water Tribe.
  • Sokka – Katara's brother, a warrior full of plans and jokes.
  • Toph – A blind earthbender who "sees" through vibrations in the ground.
  • Zuko – The banished prince of the Fire Nation.
  • Iroh – Zuko's wise uncle, who loves tea.
